# Buying Checklist

Follow these steps before making a purchase:

1. **Read the Ad Carefully:** Ensure you understand the condition and specs.
2. **Contact the Seller:** Use the provided contact method to ask any clarifying questions.
3. **Inspect the Item:** If possible, inspect the item in person before paying.
4. **Negotiate:** Don't hesitate to negotiate if the price is listed as negotiable.
5. **Stay Safe:** Meet in public places and avoid transferring money before seeing the item.
