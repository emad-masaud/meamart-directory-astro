# Pricing and Negotiation

Negotiation is a normal part of buying and selling.

## For Buyers
- **Do your research:** Know the market value of the item.
- **Be polite:** Start with a reasonable offer.
- **Ask for the final price:** Sometimes sellers have a minimum price in mind.

## For Sellers
- **Leave room:** Price your item slightly higher if you expect negotiation.
- **Be firm but flexible:** Know your lowest acceptable price.
