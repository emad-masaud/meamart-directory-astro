## What is the MeaMart Ad Agent?

The MeaMart Ad Agent helps you write a well-structured ad based on the templates in our Smart Fill guide.

## How to use the Agent with Smart Fill

1. Open the AI Assistant on MeaMart or the agent connected to your account.
2. Answer the agent's questions about your item or service:
   - Item type
   - City and district
   - Condition
   - Key specifications
   - Price
   - WhatsApp number

3. Wait until it sends you a final, ready-to-use ad text.
4. Copy the entire text.
5. Open the ad creation form on MeaMart.
6. Paste the text into the **Smart Fill** box.
7. Click the **Auto-Fill Fields** button.

The system will automatically fill in the title, description, and key fields based on what the agent wrote.

## Important Notes

- Try to keep your answers clear and concise so the agent can generate a clean ad.
- If you don't like the first draft, ask the agent to rewrite it more concisely or in a different tone.
- Always double-check the price and contact number before publishing the ad.
