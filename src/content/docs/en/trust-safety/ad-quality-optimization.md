# Ad Quality Optimization

Better ads get more views and sell faster.

## Titles and Descriptions
- **Clear Title:** Use keywords buyers will search for.
- **Detailed Description:** Answer common questions before they are asked.

## High-Quality Photos
- Upload clear, bright photos. Ads with photos get significantly more engagement.

## Accurate Categorization
- Ensure your ad is in the right category so buyers can find it easily.
