# Trust & Safety

Your safety is our priority. Please follow these guidelines:

## Safe Transactions
- **Meet in Public:** Always arrange to meet in well-lit, public areas.
- **Cash is King:** Be cautious with bank transfers, especially from unknown sources.
- **Inspect First:** Never pay before inspecting the item or verifying the property/service.

## Reporting Suspicious Activity
- If an ad looks like a scam (e.g., price is too good to be true), please report it.
- Do not share sensitive personal information like passwords or ID numbers.
