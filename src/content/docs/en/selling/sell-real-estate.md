# Sell Real Estate

A good real estate listing provides all the details a potential buyer or tenant needs.

## Essential Information
- **Property Type:** Apartment, Villa, Land, etc.
- **Status:** For Sale or For Rent.
- **Location:** City, district, and proximity to landmarks.
- **Details:** Number of rooms, bathrooms, and floor level.

## Extras that add value
Mention if the property has:
- Parking space
- Elevator
- Furnished / Unfurnished
- Balcony or View
