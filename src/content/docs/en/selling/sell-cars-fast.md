# Sell Cars Fast

Selling a car requires building trust with the buyer. Here is how to do it.

## Photos Matter
- **Exterior:** Take clear photos from all 4 angles in good daylight.
- **Interior:** Show the dashboard, seats, and odometer.
- **Flaws:** Be honest and photograph any scratches or dents.

## Clear Specifications
Make sure to include:
- Make, Model, and Year.
- Mileage (Odometer reading).
- Condition (e.g., excellent, used, needs repair).
- Engine type and Transmission.

## Pricing
- Check similar cars on the platform to set a competitive price.
- Indicate if the price is negotiable.
