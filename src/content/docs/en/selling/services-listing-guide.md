# Services Listing Guide

When offering a service, credibility is key.

## Describe Your Service
- Be clear about what exactly you offer (e.g., "Home Cleaning", "AC Repair").
- Mention your coverage areas.

## Build Trust
- State your years of experience.
- Mention if you have a portfolio or past work examples.
- Clearly state your pricing model (per hour, per project, etc.).
